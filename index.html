How we pick a workflow

  1. Name the job the team already does.
  2. Find the report inside the tools they already pay for.
  3. Separate fields that may be pasted from fields that never leave the shop.
  4. Write the human checks before any prompt.
  5. Demonstrate on SAMPLE rows only.
  6. Refuse fake hours-saved or revenue numbers.
  7. Hand over a one-page ritual they can run without us.

About

Mathew Manders

Mathew (“Matty”) Manders is the founder of Spark & Structure Consulting and a Senior Training Manager / technical enablement leader based in Aurora, IL (Chicago area). He holds a B.S. in Information Technology and an M.S. in Adult Education & Training (University of Phoenix) and has worked in IT and training since 1997.

Career highlights include training leadership roles at Relativity, Verizon Wireless, Plexus, Navistar, WMS Gaming, Clinical Computer Systems, and Amazon. His track record: taking certification pass rates from 60% to 96% (~$3M in annual savings), training 3,000+ employees, and rebuilding onboarding and technical training for organizations from startups to enterprises.

Today he helps small businesses adopt AI the way that actually works: build the workflow, train the team, verify everything.

Spark is technology, experimentation, and conceptual leaps. Structure is grounding, implementation, and turning ideas into something usable. The brand is built so both strengths can meet in the center as the practice grows.
